The 2025–26 cyclone season:
how close did the trigger come?

RSMC Nadi sent forecasts for two cyclones near Fiji: Urmil in February and Vaianu in April. We step through every forecast and check it against the trigger.

Reminder

What would have activated the framework

🌀Readiness

≥ 5,000 people in the zone forecast to get 64-kt (hurricane-force) winds, in the 120-hour forecast

🌀Action

The same, within the first 72 hours

🌧️Observational

≥ 190 mm of rain over two days, averaged over the whole country

On each forecast slide: the number of people in the 64-kt zone, and how far the 64-kt winds stayed from land. 64 knots (118 km/h) is the minimum for a Category 3 cyclone.

Summary

Vaianu came closest — and it was not close

  • Every one of the 13 forecasts had 0 people in the 64-kt zone (threshold 5,000). Vaianu's hurricane-force winds stayed 130 km or more offshore.
  • Only the uncertainty cone's worst case reached populated areas, on 6–7 April. The trigger uses the forecast track itself, so this alone does not activate.
  • Rainfall peaked at 72 mm (2-day country average), against 190 mm.
