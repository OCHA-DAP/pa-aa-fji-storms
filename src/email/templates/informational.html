Framework

The framework document is available online here. The trigger mechanism, on page X of the framework document, is:

This framework will be triggered by a tropical system that either:

  • is forecasted by RSMC Nadi to cause winds of at least 64 knots in zones where at least 5,000 people live, OR
  • is observed to cause at least 190 mm of rainfall over two consecutive days, averaged over the whole country.

Every time RSMC Nadi produces a tropical cyclone track forecast, it will be evaluated by an automated script to determine whether the trigger conditions have been met.

The framework follows a 3-stage trigger activation, each part of which is triggered based on the lead time of the points in the forecast, or the observational data:

  • Readiness trigger: If the wind field indicated by the 120-hour forecast meets the trigger conditions, the readiness trigger is activated.
  • Action trigger: If the wind field indicated by the forecast truncated up to a lead time of 72 hours meets the trigger conditions, the action trigger is activated.
  • Observational trigger: If the average rainfall over the whole country during two consecutive days meets the threshold (190 mm), the observational trigger is activated.
